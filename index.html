About me

From domain expertise to a working fleet.

My work starts with how a business actually runs: records, rules, exceptions, and the people who depend on them. I bring that knowledge into AI platform design, implementation, and day-to-day operation.

I work with developers and use AI coding assistants to build and maintain the tools. My contribution spans the business problem, the architecture, the knowledge behind the assistant, and the checks that make the result useful.

Claude helped build much of this portfolio. Codex is now part of my workflow too. The lasting assets are the documented knowledge, integrations, tests, and operating practices that a team can reuse.

A note on the names: organisations, proprietary products, and customers use Star Wars codenames. Public technology names are real. This is my own account of the work; private code and customer records stay private.

Selected work

Four views of the platform.

Holocron

Give the assistant the missing knowledge.

Project notes ↗

The problem. ERP source and conventions are too specialised to leave to a model’s general training.

My contribution. My work brings source retrieval, development documentation, and controlled assistant access into one service.

The result. Coding assistants can retrieve relevant domain material before proposing changes. Qdrant and reranking support retrieval; Microsoft 365 sign-in controls access.

Jedi Archives

Make documentation usable at the point of work.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Product answers are scattered across manuals, FAQs, API references, and other material.

My contribution. I build and operate the shared knowledge service that makes these sources searchable through MCP.

The result. The same documentation can support assistant workflows and grounded product answers, with source articles available for inspection.

Autotests Portal

Close the loop between generation and verification.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Writing a test is only useful if people can run it, inspect the result, and repeat it.

My contribution. My platform work connects test authoring, AI-assisted generation, execution, and result review in a shared portal.

The result. Developers and testers have a common workflow for exercising ERP behaviour and investigating regressions.

Customer Chatbot

Put product knowledge in the customer’s hands.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Customers need answers grounded in the product, across languages.

My contribution. I build and operate a support assistant with retrieval, citations, feedback, and a deliberately limited knowledge boundary.

The result. Product documentation feeds the assistant. Internal ERP source remains separate. Feedback helps identify where the knowledge needs improvement.
