What these numbers mean

These are dated snapshots recorded in project operations, not live totals. A chat turn is one question or lookup, not a unique person or a resolved support case. Source clicks measure engagement; they do not prove that an answer was correct or a support ticket was avoided.

The chatbot window runs from 25 September at 15:00 to approximately 13:00 on 8 October 2026. Median answer completion was 10.3 seconds; median time to the first token was 5.8 seconds. The knowledge total is a separate 20 October snapshot: indexed records may include document sections, rather than unique original articles.

Feedback is useful for finding problems, but the small, self-selected rating sample does not support a customer-satisfaction percentage.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Customers know the problem they are trying to solve. They should not need to know which manual, article, or ERP term contains the answer.

My contribution. I build and operate a support assistant around the product knowledge base: retrieval, source citations, multilingual support, feedback, and the controls needed for a public service.

The result. The recorded September–October window contains 1,142 public chat turns; 28% included a source click. Those signals help me see what people ask and where the guidance needs work.

Jedi Archives

Build the foundation behind the answer.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Useful product knowledge is spread across FAQs, manuals, references, and support material. It needs to be searchable and maintained before an assistant can use it well.

My contribution. I build and operate the shared knowledge service, from source ingestion and terminology to search and article retrieval through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

The result. The index held 43,583 records in the 20 October snapshot. The same knowledge foundation supports assistant workflows and the customer chatbot. Customer answers stay within the product-support knowledge base.

Holocron

Give developers the missing context.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Specialist ERP development conventions and guides need to be available when a developer or coding assistant needs them.

My contribution. My work brings development documentation, reusable reference material, and controlled assistant access into one service for the development workflow.

Autotests Portal

Make verification part of the workflow.

Project notes ↗

The problem. Generating a test is only useful if people can run it, inspect the result, and repeat it.

My contribution. My platform work connects test authoring, AI-assisted generation, execution, and result review in a shared portal.

The result. Developers and testers have a common workflow for exercising ERP behaviour and investigating regressions. The test run gives the team evidence to review alongside generated code.
