# Focused AI pilot

## Positioning

I help businesses connect their knowledge, systems and workflows with AI, bringing 20+ years of ERP experience to the business rules and daily work behind the solution. I guide implementation with developers and AI coding assistants.

The owner approved a focused pilot as the main offer on 1 November 2026, with advisory and ongoing support available around it. Alternatives considered were advisory alone (a roadmap that others implement) and an ongoing partnership (broader operating commitments). The pilot makes the first engagement concrete and bounded.

## Engagement

1. Understand the work with its users: process, rules, available data and baseline.
2. Choose one useful change: scope, deliverables and measures agreed before implementation.
3. Build and test a pilot with developers and AI coding assistants; the customer's team validates it against real work and business rules.
4. Review the measured results, document and hand over the pilot, then decide whether to extend it.

The deliverable is a working pilot, agreed checks, a results review and a documented handover. Scope, responsibilities and access are agreed before work begins. Advisory and continued operation require their own agreement. No price, duration, availability, saving or performance target has been invented.

## Possible applications

Agriculture: investigate field/crop costs using purchasing, fuel, labour and machinery records; prepare activity notes for review.

Transport: connect completed orders, delivery documents, work hours and invoices to identify missing paperwork and costs needing attention.

Manufacturing: search approved instructions with references and compare recorded material use against plans.

Construction: connect site reports, estimates, approved changes and actual costs to follow up extra work.

Wholesale/retail: prepare order entries for review and flag missing information or stock exceptions.

Services: connect enquiries, quotations, scheduled work, timesheets and billing to show the next action.

These are illustrative applications to scope with each business, not additional completed projects or sector qualifications. Sector specialists validate their own business requirements. The existing 32 projects and separately dated results remain the evidence of delivered work.
